# Privacy Policy

**Picture-in-Picture Fix (by CodeSlayer)**

Last updated: 11 November 2026

## Overview

Picture-in-Picture Fix is a browser extension that allows users to watch videos in Picture-in-Picture mode, including on websites that normally disable this functionality.

This extension is designed to operate locally within your browser without collecting or transmitting personal information.

## Data Collection

Picture-in-Picture Fix does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, browsing history, or website content with the developer or third parties.

The extension does not use analytics, tracking services, or advertising.

## Website Access

The extension requires access to webpages to identify video elements and enable Picture-in-Picture functionality.

Video elements are processed locally within your browser. Website content and browsing information are not transmitted to external servers by the extension.

## Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local extension storage to remember whether automatic Picture-in-Picture is enabled.

This preference is stored locally in your browser and is not transmitted to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

The extension does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data handling practices change.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
